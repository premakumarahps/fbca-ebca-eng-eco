=== PAGE 2 ===
Product: 
Prefabricated ferrocement wall panel made of a cement–
sand mortar reinforced with wire mesh, suitable for modular
walls in toilets, tanks, and housing units.
Existing Issues: 
Current prototypes at NERDC face cracking during
lifting/handling and excessive self-weight, making transport
and installation difficult.
Final Goal: 
To create an optimized, durable, and sustainable wall panel
- reducing weight, preventing cracks.
